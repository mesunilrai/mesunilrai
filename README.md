## 📌 How I Approach AI in TPM

I am interested in AI that improves **decision quality and execution**, rather than simply generating summaries.

My approach is:

```text
Real TPM Problem
      ↓
AI-assisted Analysis
      ↓
Evidence + Uncertainty
      ↓
Action / Decision Support
      ↓
Human Review
      ↓
Continuous Evaluation
```

The objective is to build tools that can be useful to both **new and experienced TPMs** while keeping human judgment and accountability in the loop.
